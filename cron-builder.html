📚 Cron Format Reference:
  • Field 1: Minute (0-59)
  • Field 2: Hour (0-23)
  • Field 3: Day of Month (1-31)
  • Field 4: Month (1-12 or JAN-DEC)
  • Field 5: Day of Week (0-6 or SUN-SAT, 0=Sunday)
