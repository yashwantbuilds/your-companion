📚 Common Regex Examples

Extract all email addresses [\w.-]+@[\w.-]+\.\w+ Matches: john@example.com, user.name@site.co.uk
Extract all numbers \d+ Matches: 123, 456, 789
Remove extra spaces \s+ → Replace with: Converts multiple spaces to single space
Extract URLs https?://[^\s]+ Matches: http://example.com, https://site.org
Phone numbers (US format) \(\d{3}\) \d{3}-\d{4} Matches: (123) 456-7890
✅ All regex processing happens locally in your browser - no data is sent to any server
